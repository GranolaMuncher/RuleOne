# 246 · New Year's Intentions

**In one sentence:** A short year-end chat in which Phil and Danielle prefer intentions to resolutions, tell listeners to use 2020 to prepare for the next sale, and argue that good sleep and low stress improve investing decisions.

## Key ideas
- **Think long term, not yearly.** Phil prefers asking what you want for your family over the long run, not setting a goal for 2020. Both dislike resolutions and prefer a few promises kept. [01:00–03:00]
- **One simple method.** Find businesses you understand, with a durable advantage and good management, and buy when on sale. [02:00–03:30]
- **Sales were rare in 2019.** Phil says bargains were hard to find in that market, but that they will come, so treat 2020 as preparation. [03:00–04:00]
- **Protect your consciousness.** Danielle's first intention is deep, unmedicated sleep, citing Matthew Walker's *Why We Sleep*. Phil likens fatigue to coloured glasses: you don't notice the tint until it's off. [04:00–07:00]
- **Better rest, better decisions.** They add yoga, meditation, prayer and walking as stress relievers and tie them to better investing. This is their opinion. [06:00–07:30]
