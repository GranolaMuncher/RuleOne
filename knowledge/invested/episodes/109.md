## Key ideas (see 084 for detail)
- **Munger's four filters** are "simple but not easy". Guy adds a fifth: forgive yourself for mistakes so you keep going. [08:00–15:00]
- **Horsehead post-mortem.** Debt crept up like the lukewarm-water frog (Munger's "24 causes"), in a commodity business. Guy now asks why management takes on debt. [15:00–19:00]
- **Judging management** means reading ten years of 10-Ks and 10-Qs, then asking questions. Reg FD means anyone can join earnings calls. Order matters: do your reading before hearing management's story. [19:00–29:00]
- **Talk businesses, not positions,** and share experience rather than advice. [30:00–36:00]
