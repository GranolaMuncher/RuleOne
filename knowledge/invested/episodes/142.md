# 142 · A Holiday Message

**In one sentence:** A short year-end message in which Phil and Danielle say wealth is about more than money, and encourage listeners to practise gratitude and giving.

## Key ideas
- **Holiday cheer is a practice.** Danielle values the one time of year when a culture remembers to be kind, give and say thank you. [01:00–02:00]
- **Gratitude as structure.** Phil says bringing gratitude into life in a structured way is good for you, ideally daily. [03:00–04:00]
- **If you can't be with family,** go and bring cheer to someone else (a care home, a hospital, a shelter, a neighbour). [04:00–05:00]
- **Wealth is more than money.** Phil says it is about living a great life, and that the practice is the journey as much as the result. [05:00]
- **As you improve, improve someone else's situation.** Start at home by teaching children to save and invest. They save more when they see it grow. [05:30–06:00]
- **Looking ahead.** It is the last episode of 2017, and 2018 promises new topics. [06:00–06:30]
