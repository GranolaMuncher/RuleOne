**In one sentence:** A rerun of [353](353.md) from January 2022. After a long opening about Swiss banking and homing pigeons, it is the same money-supply and inflation discussion, so read 353 for the content.

## Key ideas
- **Same conversation as 353.** The Fed's job, money-supply growth (M1, charted on FRED), supply bottlenecks and whether inflation is temporary. [10:00–24:00]

## Words to know
- **M1**: a measure of money supply, cash plus checking-type deposits (and savings since 2020).
- **FRED**: the St. Louis Fed's free economic data site.
