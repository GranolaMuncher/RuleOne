# 349 · Rule #1's 2021 Year in Review

**In one sentence:** A personal year-end episode: Phil and Danielle talk about a hard year (surgeries, a long illness) and how a quiet-mind practice helps you stay unstuck, with almost no investing content.

## Key ideas
- **A turbulent year is a learning year.** Phil had hospital stays and surgery; Danielle has been ill for over a year. Both treat it as growth. [00:00–03:00]
- **You are never behind.** Danielle credits her parents for the view that you can restart from where you are at any age. [03:00–05:00]
- **A practice that quiets the mind.** Phil says some practice (meditation, long-distance running or swimming, prayer, racing) lets you step back into silence. He compares athletes in "the zone" to meditators. [05:00–08:00]
- **Don't get stuck in what was.** Heraclitus's river is Phil's image: life moves on, and being caught in the past is like being held in a Grand Canyon eddy. [08:00–10:30]
- **Do it in a form you can manage.** Danielle can't meditate comfortably now, so she lies in a dark room with no stimulation: it needs no cost or technique. [11:00–13:00]
- **Investor takeaway.** Investing starts with putting your money toward things you care about, and a calm mind is useful when markets get loud. This is a light link, not a lesson from the episode.
