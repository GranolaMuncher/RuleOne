# 027 · Dharma and Being Mindful About Your Money

**In one sentence:** The planned dividend talk gets swapped for a conversation about why people don't invest (they never notice they aren't), why a promise to yourself is stronger than a goal, and how money, by buying freedom, can help you follow your purpose.

## Key ideas
- **Mindful means noticing.** Danielle defines it as being aware of what you're doing. Her point for investing: first notice that you aren't investing at all. She says she hadn't registered it until the podcast was planned. [00:00–04:00]
- **Most people aren't refusing, they're not looking.** Even Phil's own literary agent read *Rule #1* and *Payback Time* and changed nothing. Disinterest is the default, not a character flaw. [04:00–07:00]
- **Crisis forces the issue.** Phil compares it to a doctor's warning about your heart: baby boomers around 63 with $100k suddenly notice. Better to act before the crisis. [03:00–08:00]
- **Find the version you won't hate.** Like exercise, the first goal is a form of investing you can keep doing, and liking it is a bonus. Danielle's riding and yoga count as exercise she never logged, so you may already be doing more than you notice. [07:00–11:00]
- **A promise beats a goal.** Phil promised himself to work hard for five years and make a million. He says a goal is an intention that lets you off the hook, while a promise puts your character on the line. Danielle isn't sure the word changes behaviour: some people may respond to goals. [13:00–19:00]
- **"Burn the ships."** Full commitment tends to attract unexpected help. Danielle adds the check: decide whether obstacles are just obstacles or a sign you're going the wrong way. [18:00–22:00]
- **Dharma as flow.** Doing what you love, doing it well and being useful is Phil's picture of purpose, shown by a river guide who rows a heavy boat with little effort because her attention is trained. Mindfulness plus skill means less effort. [22:00–26:00]
- **Pushback: purpose isn't a test you can fail.** Danielle says it's fine to have a job you don't love, and not everyone should be a full-time investor. Phil agrees there's a "weekend warrior" level. [26:00–28:00]
- **Paths curve.** Phil's route (Special Forces, river guide, investor, author, speaker) was unplannable. Burnout was the signal to change, and he had to notice the opening and be brave. [29:00–34:00]
- **The bridge to money.** A source of wealth beyond a salary gives freedom, and freedom gives choices. That is Danielle's own reason to invest. Dividends and the equity bond are postponed to next time. [27:00–29:00, 34:00–36:00]

## Words to know
- **Dharma:** Phil's use is "what you're here to do", where skill, enjoyment and usefulness overlap.
- **Mindful:** being aware of what you're doing, with no mysticism required.
- **Promise vs goal:** a promise stakes your word on the outcome, a goal is an intention.
