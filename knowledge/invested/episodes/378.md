# 378 · Netflix Questions

**In one sentence:** A short solo episode from Danielle that recaps the listener questions left over from the Netflix series (debt, free cash flow, bundling, culture, account sharing) as things to watch on the earnings call.

## Key ideas
- **Questions are a learning tool.** Danielle finds it useful to carry open questions that nobody can answer yet. [00:00–01:00]
- **Watch how management talks.** Leaders who discuss real problems candidly are more trustworthy with your money than those who spin everything. She plans to watch the Q2 earnings interview for this. [01:00–03:00]
- **Debt and free cash flow.** Still the main question: when does Netflix turn free-cash-flow positive? [03:00]
- **Bundling.** Amazon, Disney, and Apple could bundle other services' content and compete in a way Netflix doesn't. [03:00–04:00]
- **Free alternatives.** YouTube is free, and subscription fatigue is real, so any paid service competes with whatever new free option arrives. [04:00]
- **Culture questions.** A listener asks whether Ted Sarandos' quantity-over-quality approach created a fear culture; Danielle's point is that the founder may be excellent while day-to-day leadership still needs investigation. She invites insider knowledge. [05:00–06:00]
- **Price and ads.** Netflix is the most expensive service and is adding ads, hinting at brand fragility. [05:00–06:00]
- **Account sharing.** Cracking down goes against the cord-cutting mentality; rivals may let Netflix absorb the reputational hit first. [06:00–07:00]
