# 393 · Timing Tee-Up

**In one sentence:** In a short solo episode Danielle reads a listener's neat summary of her clash with Phil in [392](392.md) (Buffett's "hold forever" versus dancing in and out of a stock) and poses two questions for the next episode.

## Key ideas
- **Twitter as a case study.** Danielle says she once looked at Twitter and liked its board of directors, which has since been replaced; she calls it a vivid example of the Buffett saying to buy a business a monkey could run, because someday a monkey will. (Her paraphrase of a Buffett remark.) [01:00–02:30]
- **Rob Hall's summary.** Danielle has absorbed Buffett's "forever" holding period; Phil talks about moving in and out of a company several times in a decade; she sees that as crystal-ball market timing; Phil sees it as responding to his situation as a smaller investor. [02:30–04:00]
- **Question one.** How can intrinsic value guide when to get in and out, and how is that different from timing the market? [03:30–04:15]
- **Question two.** How do you know when to switch back to a hold-forever mindset? [04:00–04:30]
- **Her hunch.** The answer is probably "it depends" and gets clearer with experience, which may be why Phil struggles to state rules for what feels obvious to him. [04:30–05:00]
