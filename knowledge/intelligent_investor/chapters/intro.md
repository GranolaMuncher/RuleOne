# Introduction

> **Preview.** Written from general knowledge to guide your reading. It's replaced by full study notes once you record this chapter.

## What to listen for
- Graham separates the **defensive** (passive) investor, who wants safety and freedom from effort, from the **enterprising** (active) investor, who will put in time for better results.
- The book is about investment *principles and attitudes*, not about forecasting markets or picking hot stocks.
- Watch for his warning that most investors' problem and worst enemy is likely to be themselves.
- Zweig's commentary updates the dot-com era lessons. Listen for his examples of 'can't-miss' growth stories that missed.
