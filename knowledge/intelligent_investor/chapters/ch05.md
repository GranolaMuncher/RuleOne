# Chapter 5 · The Defensive Investor and Common Stocks

> **Preview.** Written from general knowledge to guide your reading. It's replaced by full study notes once you record this chapter.

## What to listen for
- Graham's rules for the defensive investor's stocks: **adequate diversification**, large and prominent conservatively financed companies, a **long dividend record**, and a price limit tied to earnings.
- **Dollar-cost averaging**, buying a fixed amount regularly, is a way to remove the urge to time.
- Listen for his caution about 'growth stocks': the risk is paying too much for growth everyone already sees.
- Rule #1 link: compare these rules with the Big Five and Town's tranche buying.
