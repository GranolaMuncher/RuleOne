# Chapter 13 · A Comparison of Four Listed Companies

> **Preview.** Written from general knowledge to guide your reading. It's replaced by full study notes once you record this chapter.

## What to listen for
- A worked comparison of four listed companies: how the same analysis gives very different verdicts.
- Listen for the measures Graham uses: profitability, stability, growth, financial position, dividends and price history.
- Notice how a popular, high-P/E company can be the riskiest of the four.
- Try comparing four companies in one industry on the All stocks page the same way.
