# Chapter 6 · Portfolio Policy for the Enterprising Investor: Negative Approach

> **Preview.** Written from general knowledge to guide your reading. It's replaced by full study notes once you record this chapter.

## What to listen for
- What the enterprising investor should **avoid**: second-grade bonds and preferreds, foreign government bonds and new issues (IPOs).
- IPOs are sold when sellers think the timing is best for them, not for you.
- Listen for the idea that above-average results come from avoiding mistakes as much as from brilliant picks.
- Zweig's IPO and dot-com examples.
