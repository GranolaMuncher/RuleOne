# Chapter 20 · “Margin of Safety” as the Central Concept of Investment

> **Preview.** Written from general knowledge to guide your reading. It's replaced by full study notes once you record this chapter.

## What to listen for
- **Margin of safety**, the central concept: buy at a price far enough below value that errors and bad luck don't cause losses.
- For bonds and stocks alike, the margin comes from earning power well above what's required, or from price well below value.
- Graham ends with 'investment is most intelligent when it is most businesslike'.
- Rule #1 link: this is where the 50% MOS comes from. Town applies it to Sticker Price, Graham more to assets and earnings power.
