## What to listen for
- Why reported **per-share earnings** can mislead: special charges, accounting choices and dilution.
- Graham's advice is to use **average earnings over several years**, not one year.
- Listen for how one company's 'record earnings' hid accounting quirks.
- RuleOne link: the screen uses multi-year medians for growth and P/E for the same reason.
