## What to listen for
- Investment funds (mutual funds, closed-end funds): costs, performance and how to choose.
- Graham finds most funds don't beat the market after costs.
- Listen for when closed-end funds at a discount are attractive.
- Zweig strongly favours low-cost index funds for anyone who doesn't want to do the work.
