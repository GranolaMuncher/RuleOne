> **Preview.** Written from general knowledge to guide your reading. It's replaced by full study notes once you record this chapter.

## What to listen for
- Eight pairs of companies side by side, showing how quality and price combine.
- Often the cheaper-looking company is the better investment, and sometimes the 'quality' company is fairly priced.
- Listen for how Graham weighs balance-sheet strength against growth.
- Try pairing two competitors on the site and writing your own comparison.
