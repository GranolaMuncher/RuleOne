# Chapter 1 · Investment versus Speculation: Results to Be Expected by the Intelligent Investor

> **Preview.** Written from general knowledge to guide your reading. It's replaced by full study notes once you record this chapter.

## What to listen for
- Graham's definition: an investment operation, after thorough analysis, promises **safety of principal and an adequate return**. Anything else is speculation.
- Speculation isn't evil, but **mixing it up with investing** is. Listen for his advice to keep any speculating in a small, separate account.
- Expected results differ for the defensive investor (a simple stock/bond split) and the enterprising investor (more work, more return only if done well).
- Zweig on day trading and market timing: why trading frequently and chasing performance destroy returns.
