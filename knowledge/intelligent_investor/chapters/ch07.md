# Chapter 7 · Portfolio Policy for the Enterprising Investor: The Positive Side

> **Preview.** Written from general knowledge to guide your reading. It's replaced by full study notes once you record this chapter.

## What to listen for
- Where the enterprising investor can look: **relatively unpopular large companies**, bargain issues (including stocks below net current assets) and special situations.
- Graham's skepticism of trying to beat the market through growth-stock forecasting.
- Listen for 'net-nets' (buying below net working capital) and why they worked statistically.
- Rule #1 link: unpopular large companies are close to Town's 'wonderful company hit by an event'.
