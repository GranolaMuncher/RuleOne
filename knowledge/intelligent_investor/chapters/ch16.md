# Chapter 16 · Convertible Issues and Warrants

> **Preview.** Written from general knowledge to guide your reading. It's replaced by full study notes once you record this chapter.

## What to listen for
- Convertible bonds, convertible preferreds and warrants. Graham is generally skeptical of them for ordinary investors.
- Warrants in particular dilute shareholders and serve speculation.
- This is the closest the book gets to options. It's consistent with this project's 'no options' choice.
- Zweig updates it for modern convertibles.
