# Chapter 2 · The Investor and Inflation

> **Preview.** Written from general knowledge to guide your reading. It's replaced by full study notes once you record this chapter.

## What to listen for
- Whether stocks protect against inflation better than bonds. Graham's answer is 'partly, not reliably'.
- He argues against putting everything in stocks just because inflation is feared.
- Listen for the link between corporate profitability (return on capital) and how well stocks keep up with rising prices.
- Zweig adds TIPS and real assets as modern inflation hedges.
