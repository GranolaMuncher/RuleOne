# Chapter 11 · Security Analysis for the Lay Investor: General Approach

> **Preview.** Written from general knowledge to guide your reading. It's replaced by full study notes once you record this chapter.

## What to listen for
- The core of security analysis: how an analyst values a stock using **future earning power, financial strength, dividend record** and quality of management.
- Graham's simple growth-stock valuation formula (a base P/E plus a multiple of expected growth) comes with heavy caveats.
- Listen for why projections are uncertain and why the margin of safety has to absorb that uncertainty.
- Rule #1 link: compare it with Sticker Price (EPS × growth × future P/E, discounted at 15%).
