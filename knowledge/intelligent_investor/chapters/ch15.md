> **Preview.** Written from general knowledge to guide your reading. It's replaced by full study notes once you record this chapter.

## What to listen for
- How the enterprising investor selects stocks: a looser set of criteria, more work and looking for bargains the market overlooks.
- Listen for net-current-asset bargains and his small-company screens.
- Graham notes that by the 1970s such bargains were rarer, but they reappear in panics.
- Rule #1 link: compare with the Ten Cap and Payback Time prices.
